An intimate, romantic, and cinematic interactive birthday experience created as a private surprise for **NAJIYA**.

Designed like a moving storybook:
**Mystery → Curiosity → Cute/Fun → Memories → Feelings → Surprise → Emotional Message → Grand Finale ❤️**

Built with **100% Pure Vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript** — zero frameworks, zero external dependencies, and completely offline-ready.

## ✨ Features & Cinematic Stages

### 🔐 1. Secret Intro (Mystery & Suspense)

- Opens in complete darkness with twinkling stars and gentle fireflies.
- Sequential storytelling pauses:
  - *"Hey... 👀"*
  - *"I made something for you."*
  - *"It's not just a birthday wish..."*
  - *"It's a little journey."*
- Glowing button: **`OPEN YOUR SURPRISE ✨`** with ambient particle bursts.

### 💌 2. Personal Birthday Invitation

- Fullscreen midnight romantic aesthetic.
- *"For Someone Special ❤️"* and grand serif title for **NAJIYA**.
- Subtle slow-drifting starlight and warm candlelight aura.

### 📖 3. Our Little Story (Interactive Timeline)

- Horizontal & vertical timeline:
  - ✦ **The Beginning**: *"Some people enter your life unexpectedly..."*
  - ✦ **The Conversations**: *"Some conversations become memories."*
  - ✦ **The Random Moments**: *"The smallest moments sometimes become the most memorable ones."*
  - ✦ **The Smile**: *"And somehow... your smile became one of those things I remember."*

### 📸 4. Memory Gallery & Fullscreen Lightbox

- High-definition cards with 3D tilt hover physics and heartfelt captions.
- Interactive favorite heart buttons with particle bursts.
- **Fullscreen Lightbox**: Tap any photo to inspect it in high resolution with touch swipe gestures on mobile!

### ❤️ 5. "10 Little Things I Like About You"

- Cute, charming, and flirty without being overwhelming.
- Displays one card at a time with a dynamic progress bar:
  - *Your smile.*
  - *The way you randomly make conversations interesting.*
  - *Your little reactions 😂*
  - *The way you are simply... you.*
  - *Your kindness...*
- Concluding with: *"Honestly... I could keep going. But let's save some secrets for later. 😉"*

### 🎮 6. Playful Friendship / Crush Quiz

- Lighthearted questions that make her laugh:
  - *"Who is more likely to reply late? 😂"*
  - *"Nee thaththiii thane ? (Be honest 😂)"*
  - *"Eptiyum pesa matta athane ? 🤐"*
  - *"Who has the cutest random reactions? ✨"*
- Animated feedback: Emerald checkmark for correct answers, playful shake animation for wrong choices.
- Percentage score celebration (e.g. *100% — "Okay... you know yourself pretty well! 😂"*).

### 🎂 7. Make a Wish (Interactive Cake)

- Prompts her to type a secret birthday wish: *"Write your birthday wish... 🤫"*
- Interactive 3D birthday cake with glowing flickering candles.
- **Tap to Blow Out Candles**: Extinguishes the flame with realistic smoke puffs, confetti, and romantic chime!
- Message: *"I hope that wish comes true. ❤️"*

### 🎁 8. Mystery 3D Gift Box

- Spotlighted 3D wrapped golden gift package with fluttering ribbon.
- Tap to unwrap: Box shakes, lid pops open with a burst of light & confetti, revealing the special memory photo with:
  *"Some memories are too special to stay hidden."*

### 💌 9. Secret Wax-Sealed Tamil Letter

- Authentic 3D envelope with a red wax seal stamp.
- Tap to crack open: Background music softens, rose petals drift down, and a real-time **typewriter effect** recites the Tamil poem:
  ```text
  உன் வார்த்தைகளால் வரும் கோவமும்,
  உன் பார்வையால் எழும் கேள்விக்கு
  பதில் தேடுகிறேன் உந்தன் மௌனத்தில்...!!
  ```
- Signed with warmth: *"Happy Birthday, Najiya ❤️ — I hope this little surprise made you smile."*

### 🌌 10. One Last Secret (Countdown)

- Fades softly to darkness.
- Sequential pauses:
  - *"Wait..."*
  - *"One last thing."*
  - *"Look at the sky. ✨"*
- Starry sky awakens with a cinematic **3... 2... 1** countdown.

### 🎆 11. Grand Finale Fireworks & Climax

- Fullscreen Canvas Fireworks engine with realistic physics, trails, and sparks.
- **Tap anywhere on the sky** to shoot custom fireworks!
- Shining typography: **HAPPY BIRTHDAY NAJIYA ❤️**
- Pulsing beating 3D heart with energy rings.
- The emotional climax quote:
  ```text
  Some people become memories.
  Some become stories.
  And some...
  just become a little more special
  than they were supposed to. ❤️

  Happy Birthday, Najiya 🎂
  Made especially for you.
  ```

### 💖 Secret Easter Egg (Hidden Interaction)

- Tap the top-right heart icon **5 times**:
  Unlocks the secret badge modal:
  *"🔓 SECRET UNLOCKED: Okay... You weren't supposed to find this! 😂 But since you did... You're officially one of my favourite people. ❤️"*

### 🎵 Procedural Web Audio Synthesizer

- Built using the native browser **Web Audio API**.
- Synthesizes dreamy ambient chords, a romantic birthday lullaby, and sound effects (clicks, chimes, whooshes, typewriter ticks, firework booms).
- **Zero MP3 files needed** — works 100% offline and will never fail to load.
- Sound On/Off toggle at top-right.
